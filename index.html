Why EduNova

Built around how schools actually work

Most schools don't struggle because they lack tools — they struggle because their tools don't talk to each other. Attendance lives in one spreadsheet, grades in another, fees in a third system entirely, and by the time anyone needs a full picture of a student, half the morning is gone piecing it together.

EduNova was designed to close that gap. Every module — attendance, exams, fees, library, transport — reads from the same student record, so a change made by one staff member is instantly visible to everyone else who needs it: teachers, administrators, and parents alike. Nothing is duplicated, and nothing gets lost between departments.

The result is a platform that feels less like ten different apps stitched together, and more like a single, calm system that grows with the school — from a single campus with a few hundred students, to a multi-branch institution managing thousands.

FAQ

Common questions from schools

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and our team will walk you through it.

How long does it take to set up EduNova?

Most single-campus schools are fully live within one to two weeks. Our onboarding team imports your existing student, staff, and fee records, and configures classes and roles alongside you.

Can we migrate data from our current system or spreadsheets?

Yes. We accept CSV and Excel exports for students, staff, and financial records, and our team validates the data before it goes live to avoid duplicates or mismatches.

Does EduNova support multiple branches or campuses?

Yes. Larger institutions can manage several campuses from one account, with separate classes, staff, and fee structures per branch, while administrators keep a unified view across all of them.

Do parents get their own access?

Every plan includes a parent portal where guardians can view attendance, grades, fee balances, and school announcements for each of their children, without needing to contact the school directly.

Is our school's data kept private and secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is controlled by role-based permissions, and each school's records are logically isolated from every other school on the platform.

What kind of support is included after launch?

All plans include email and chat support during business hours, with priority response times and a dedicated account manager on the higher-tier plans.
